# SafelyGo visual direction

Use the visual language of a campus utility: legible controls, a strong primary action and calm secondary information. Respect the user's blue/white preference, rejection of orange, editorial styling, decorative images and artificial depth.

Tokens: navy #18243B for primary walk surfaces and text; sapphire #3157A5 for actions; white #FFFFFF for controls; ice #F6F7FB for the canvas; lavender tint #EDF0FA for selection; slate #56627A for supporting text. Red remains reserved for urgent or failed states.

System sans-serif throughout; 26-pixel screen titles, 20-pixel section titles, 16-pixel body/actions, 14-pixel supporting details. Left alignment, explicit labels and sufficient wrapping at 320 pixels.

Home: brand/account header → greeting → campus updates when present → one navy walk panel with a contrasting white action → one white group containing Map, Report and Help → brief emergency guidance. Map puts map interaction ahead of optional search. Forms remain plain with consistent bordered secondary controls.

Review against the brief: the previous uniform white-card treatment had too little hierarchy. Spend contrast on the walk panel, not decorative gradients or shadows. Avoid extra status badges, drawings, photos, serif displays and all-cap labels. Icons identify actual functions. Displayed status must reflect measured data.

October 6 refinement: retain the existing six-color system and system font. Spend visual detail on useful selection and progression: trusted people become checkbox rows with a quiet selected tint, title and address; choices get a check symbol; setup uses one shared count/progress indicator. Keep Home's core actions ahead of optional announcements. Reject extra decorative status cards and repeated questions: the screen subtitle owns the current instruction, while the form supplies concrete field labels. Review: these details encode who has access and how much work remains, specific to safety sharing and campus reporting rather than ornamental decoration.

October 6 visual guidance revision (supersedes the earlier no-image direction): Home is four large SVG action tiles, with a navy walk tile and three quiet white choices. Each pictogram depicts its function, accompanied by a short verb label. Walk shows a phone sending a position to trusted people. Reports use six illustrated categories and a check mark plus border for selection. Walk, report and campus registration use illustrated stage indicators. Review screens use labeled icon rows instead of paragraphs. Generated campus photography is limited to the sign-in banner, never presented as a real campus or proof of safety. No orange, shadows, editorial typography or mascots.
